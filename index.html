HOW TO PLAY BOXED IN

1

1. Claim edges.

Take turns selecting unclaimed edges on the 3D lattice. With confirmation on, click once to preview and click again to lock it in.

2

2. Complete all 12 edges surrounding a cube.

Each 1×1×1 cube has 12 edges. Hover any edge to see how many cubes it touches, completes, or leaves at 11/12.

3

3. Capture cubes to score points.

Claiming the 12th edge captures the cube! It fills with translucent color so you can still see inside the board. One edge can capture up to 4 cubes at once.

4

4. Completing a cube grants another turn.

Capture one or more cubes and you immediately take another move! Chain multiple captures together to swing the match.

5

5. Most cubes wins.

When all cubes are claimed, the player holding the highest cube count and board control percentage wins!

Strategy Tip — Watch for Vulnerable Cubes: Avoid leaving a cube with 11 of 12 edges unless forced — your opponent will capture it and chain extra turns.
